CEO message

Thank you for visiting Yucheon International.

Yucheon International was founded in 2019 as a water treatment equipment specialist.

Building on fiber disc filter technology developed since 1988, we supply YDF and YDFS fiber disc filters, MDF micro disc filters, filter cloth and spare parts.

Technology proven at over 320 sites in Korea now serves sewage plants and industrial sites in India, the Middle East, Europe and Southeast Asia.

We established Yucheon India in Pune in 2023 and Yucheon Corporation in the USA in 2024.

Our Talegaon factory in India is scheduled for completion in 2027.

Local production and local technical support bring us closer to you.

Yeonuu Moon, CEO, Yucheon International
